# Decision 001 — Engineering-First, AI-Enabled Company

**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Decision Owner:** Madhukumar Rajanala

## Context

Forcing AI into every client solution may create unnecessary complexity, cost, and risk.

## Decision

Invara Labs will be an **engineering-first, AI-enabled product engineering company**.

## Rationale

Engineering judgement, product understanding, reliability, and long-term maintainability remain the foundation. AI will be applied when it measurably improves quality, speed, consistency, insight, or client outcomes.

## Consequences

- AI is a core capability, not a marketing gimmick.
- Client problems determine technology choices.
- Human engineers retain responsibility for final outcomes.
- AI capabilities improve through real delivery experience.
