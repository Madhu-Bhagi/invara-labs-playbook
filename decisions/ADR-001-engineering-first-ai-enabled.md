# ADR-001 — Engineering First, AI Enabled

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 27 July 2026

## Context

Invara Labs considered positioning itself as an AI-first company. AI is strategically important, but forcing AI into every offering could weaken engineering credibility and make the company dependent on a temporary market label.

## Decision

Invara Labs will position itself as:

> **Engineering First. AI Enabled. Business Focused.**

AI will be used where it improves business outcomes, engineering quality, delivery speed, knowledge access, or operational efficiency.

## Consequences

- Engineering judgement remains central.
- AI is an enabling capability rather than the company's entire identity.
- Solutions may be AI-powered, partially automated, or non-AI depending on the problem.
- Marketing must not overstate AI usage.
- Engineers remain accountable for quality and outcomes.
