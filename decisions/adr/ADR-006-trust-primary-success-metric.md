# ADR-006 — Trust as the Primary Measure of Success

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 27 July 2026  
**Owner:** Founder

---

# Context

While defining the long-term vision of Invara Labs, several possible measures of success were considered, including:

- Revenue growth
- Company valuation
- Team size
- Number of clients
- Market share
- Technology leadership

During the discussion, it became clear that none of these represented the founder's true ambition.

Instead, the founder consistently described a company that clients would trust with their most important business and technology challenges.

Success was repeatedly expressed through statements such as:

- "They're the first company we call when we have a difficult problem."
- "They never overpromise."
- "Their engineering quality is exceptional."
- "Working with them changed our business."
- "They're the most trusted technology partner we've had."

These statements all point to a common theme:

**Trust is the outcome that defines long-term success.**

---

# Decision

Invara Labs will treat **trust** as its primary measure of long-term success.

Trust is earned through consistent behaviour rather than marketing claims.

Every strategic decision should strengthen or protect the trust placed in the company by clients, employees, and partners.

Revenue, growth, reputation, and market recognition are expected to follow as consequences of sustained trust rather than being pursued at the expense of it.

---

# What Trust Means at Invara Labs

Trust is built through five reinforcing pillars:

## 1. Integrity

We communicate honestly, set realistic expectations, and keep our commitments.

---

## 2. Engineering Excellence

We deliver reliable, maintainable, secure, and scalable solutions that reflect sound engineering judgement.

---

## 3. Business Impact

We focus on solving meaningful business problems rather than simply delivering software or technology.

---

## 4. Long-Term Partnership

We seek enduring relationships based on collaboration, transparency, and shared success.

---

## 5. Continuous Improvement

We continuously improve our people, systems, engineering practices, and use of AI to deliver increasing value over time.

---

# Implications

This decision influences every area of the company.

## Sales

Never oversell or promise outcomes that cannot be delivered.

## Engineering

Prioritise quality, maintainability, and reliability over shortcuts.

## Product

Build solutions that create lasting value rather than short-term excitement.

## AI

Apply AI where it genuinely improves outcomes instead of following trends.

## Hiring

Recruit people whose character, judgement, and professionalism reinforce client trust.

## Leadership

Make decisions that strengthen long-term credibility, even when short-term opportunities are attractive.

---

# Success Indicators

Invara Labs is succeeding when clients describe the company as:

- The first partner they contact for important challenges.
- Honest and transparent.
- Dependable under pressure.
- Technically excellent.
- Easy to work with.
- A long-term strategic partner.

These indicators are considered more meaningful than short-term financial metrics alone.

---

# Consequences

## Positive

- Establishes a clear long-term decision-making principle.
- Encourages sustainable growth.
- Builds stronger client relationships.
- Reinforces engineering quality.
- Creates a consistent company culture.

## Risks

- Choosing trust over short-term revenue may occasionally result in slower growth.
- Some opportunities may be declined when they conflict with the company's principles.

These trade-offs are considered acceptable because they protect the company's long-term reputation.

# Review

This decision should be reviewed only if the company's long-term purpose fundamentally changes.

Until then, trust remains the primary measure of success for Invara Labs.
