# ADR-002 — Systems over Heroes

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 27 July 2026

## Context

Software organisations often become dependent on a small number of individuals. This creates delivery risk, knowledge concentration, burnout, and inconsistent quality.

## Decision

Invara Labs will design its delivery model around strong systems, shared standards, collaborative teams, documentation, automation, and reusable knowledge.

## Consequences

- Knowledge must be documented and shared.
- Critical work should not depend on one person.
- Delivery practices should be repeatable.
- Reusable components and accelerators should improve over time.
- AI may assist teams, but it does not replace engineering responsibility.
