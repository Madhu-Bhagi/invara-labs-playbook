# ADR-003 — Technology Solutions Company

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 27 July 2026

## Context

Describing Invara Labs only as a software development company would limit its identity to one delivery mechanism.

The founder wants the company to solve real problems using technical expertise, which may involve software, AI, consulting, architecture, automation, modernisation, or future products.

## Decision

Invara Labs will identify as an engineering and technology solutions company.

## Consequences

- Service offerings may evolve without changing the company's purpose.
- Market messaging must focus on client problems and outcomes.
- Software development remains a core capability, not the complete identity.
- Future platforms and SaaS products fit naturally within the company direction.
