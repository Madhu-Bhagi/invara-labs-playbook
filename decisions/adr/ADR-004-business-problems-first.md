# ADR-004 — Business Problems First

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 27 July 2026

## Context

Technology companies can become focused on tools, frameworks, and trends instead of the underlying client problem.

## Decision

Invara Labs will begin every engagement by understanding the business problem, desired outcome, constraints, users, risks, and long-term operating context.

Technology will be selected only after the problem is sufficiently understood.

## Consequences

- Discovery becomes a core capability.
- Technology choices must be justified.
- Simpler solutions are preferred when they meet the need.
- Success is measured by business value and client outcomes.
- Teams must communicate in business as well as technical terms.
