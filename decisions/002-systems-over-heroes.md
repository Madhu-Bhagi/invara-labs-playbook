# Decision 002 — Systems Over Individual Heroes

**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Decision Owner:** Madhukumar Rajanala

## Context

Dependence on a few individuals creates knowledge silos, inconsistent quality, delivery risk, and burnout.

## Decision

Invara Labs will design delivery around strong systems, documented standards, intelligent automation, shared ownership, and collaborative teams.

## Consequences

- Knowledge must be documented and shared.
- Reusable standards and templates are preferred.
- Every project should improve the delivery system.
- Hiring will value systems thinking and collaboration.
- AI will amplify team capability rather than replace accountability.
