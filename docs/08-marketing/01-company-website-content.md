## Website Goal

Turn a relevant visitor into a qualified conversation by answering four questions quickly:

1. Who is Invara Labs for?
2. Which problems does it solve?
3. Why is its approach credible and different?
4. What should the visitor do next?

The website must not imply customers, certifications, geographic scale, technology expertise, or measured outcomes that Invara Labs cannot yet prove.

## Engineering Excellence Assessment

**Anchor:** `engineering-excellence-assessment`

### Best for

Technology leaders who see delivery, architecture, quality, security, or operational concerns but need independent evidence and priorities before acting.

### What you receive

- Executive findings and decision summary.
- Evidence-linked risks and strengths within agreed scope.
- Architecture and code-health observations.
- Prioritised 30-, 60-, and 90-day recommendations.
- Leadership readout and next-step options.

### Engagement shape

1–2 weeks. Fixed fee after qualification and scope confirmation.

### Boundary

This is not a certification, penetration test, full codebase review, or implementation programme unless separately scoped.

**CTA:** Discuss an assessment → `/contact?service=assessment`

## Modernisation Accelerator

**Anchor:** `modernisation-accelerator`

### Best for

Leaders whose valuable software is constrained by unsupported technology, accumulated complexity, fragile architecture, or risky delivery.

### What you receive

- Current-state baseline and constraints.
- Target architecture and decision records.
- Sequenced modernisation roadmap.
- Risk, coexistence, and rollback considerations.
- A pilot or reference slice when contracted.
- Validation findings and handover.

### Engagement shape

Paid discovery of 1–2 weeks, followed by a separately approved pilot that typically runs 2–6 weeks.

### Boundary

We do not prescribe a rewrite, cloud migration, microservices, or framework replacement before evidence supports it.

**CTA:** Discuss modernisation → `/contact?service=modernisation`

## AI Engineering Adoption Sprint

**Anchor:** `ai-engineering-adoption-sprint`

### Best for

Engineering leaders who need one team to use AI more consistently without compromising human review, sensitive information, policy, or measurable outcomes.

### What you receive

- Use-case and risk assessment.
- Pilot workflow with human-verification controls.
- Context and data-handling guidance for the agreed scope.
- Team enablement and reusable working guidance.
- Pilot evidence and outcome summary.
- Recommendation to scale, revise, restrict, or stop.

### Engagement shape

2–4 weeks for one team and a bounded set of use cases. Fixed fee after tool, policy, team, and use-case qualification.

### Boundary

This is not legal or compliance certification, vendor endorsement, autonomous engineering, or a production AI-product build.

**CTA:** Discuss an AI adoption sprint → `/contact?service=ai-adoption`

# Voice and Claim Rules

- Lead with the buyer's problem and decision, not Invara Labs capabilities.
- Use direct, calm language. Avoid hype, superlatives, and unsupported transformation claims.
- Say AI-assisted or responsibly applied AI, not AI-native or AI-first.
- Say engineering excellence company; do not imply that consulting time is absent from the commercial model.
- Name only verified technology expertise relevant to a service or proof asset.
- Do not promise faster delivery, lower cost, higher productivity, or reduced risk without a baseline and evidence method.
- Do not use the working Advisory, Framework, or Accelerators names as registered marks.

# V1 Build Acceptance Criteria

- All five routes render on mobile and desktop.
- Every page has one clear primary action.
- Navigation and footer links work.
- Contact submission has validation, error, success, spam protection, and a tested delivery destination.

- No analytics or marketing cookies are enabled without an explicit privacy decision.
