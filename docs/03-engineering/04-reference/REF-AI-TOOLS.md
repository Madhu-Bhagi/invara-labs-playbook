# AI Tools Reference

## Purpose

Provide the authoritative, changeable catalogue of AI services, models, capabilities, approved use cases, information classifications, configurations, restrictions, owners, review dates, and revocation paths.

## Planned Contents

- Tool and capability identity.
- Approved users and engineering purposes.
- Permitted and prohibited information.
- Retention, training, deletion, and residency.
- Connectors, repositories, network, browsing, and execution permissions.
- Security, privacy, legal, licensing, and customer constraints.
- Known limitations and mandatory verification.
- Approval, expiry, review triggers, incident contact, and revocation.

## Status

🔴 Planned

The catalogue must not be populated until tool ownership and approval authority are established. Until then, no tool is approved merely because it appears in this planned document.
