## Engineering excellence for software-intensive businesses

Invara Labs helps technology leaders make high-stakes engineering decisions, modernise critical software, and adopt AI responsibly. We combine senior technical judgement, evidence-led delivery, and reusable engineering methods to turn complex technology problems into clear, accountable action.

> **Engineering First. AI Enabled. Business Focused.**

## Who we help

We work with CTOs, engineering leaders, and founders at B2B product companies where software quality and delivery capability materially affect growth, risk, or customer trust.

Invara Labs is a strong fit when:

- Delivery has slowed as the product and team have grown.
- A valuable product needs a safe modernisation path.
- Architecture, quality, security, or operational risk is affecting a business decision.
- AI tools are spreading without consistent controls or measurable outcomes.
- Leadership needs an independent technical view before funding a larger change.

## Flagship services

### Engineering Excellence Assessment

An independent, evidence-based view of material engineering risks and strengths.

**Typical duration:** 1-2 weeks  
**You receive:** executive findings, evidence-linked risks, architecture and code-health observations, and a prioritised 30-, 60-, and 90-day action plan.

### Modernisation Accelerator

A practical route from a constrained legacy product to a defensible target direction and bounded pilot.

**Typical structure:** 1-2 week discovery followed by an optional 2-6 week pilot  
**You receive:** current-state baseline, target architecture, decision records, sequenced roadmap, risk and rollback considerations, and pilot evidence when contracted.

### AI Engineering Adoption Sprint

A governed way for one engineering team to test AI-assisted workflows without surrendering security, verification, or human accountability.

**Typical duration:** 2-4 weeks  
**You receive:** use-case and risk assessment, pilot workflow, data-handling guidance, team enablement, outcome evidence, and a scale/revise/restrict/stop recommendation.

## How we work

1. **Qualify** the business problem, sponsor, urgency, access, and desired decision.
2. **Focus** the engagement with explicit scope, evidence, responsibilities, and exclusions.
3. **Examine** systems and workflows with the accountable client team.
4. **Decide** using findings, trade-offs, and a sequenced recommendation.
5. **Enable** client ownership through practical artefacts and knowledge transfer.
