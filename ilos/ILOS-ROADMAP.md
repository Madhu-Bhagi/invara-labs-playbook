## Priorities

1. Keep the control layer stable.
2. Return focus to approved company and engineering content.
3. Author the Requirements Playbook when prioritised.
4. Add automation only after manual governance becomes burdensome.

## Change Rule

New roadmap items require a demonstrated repository need. Structural changes require an ADR.
