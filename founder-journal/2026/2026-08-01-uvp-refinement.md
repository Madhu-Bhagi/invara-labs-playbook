# UVP Refinement: 1 August 2026

## Context

The proposed standalone UVP included useful sales language but repeated already rejected AI-native positioning and made broad outcome claims without evidence.

## Decision

The Company Strategy Pack remains the single source of truth for the UVP. It now contains the master UVP, customer problem, intended outcomes, positioning statement, homepage message, pitch variants, proposal introduction, proof requirements, and claim boundaries.

Invara Labs will position itself as an engineering excellence company that builds client capability rather than dependency. It will not describe itself as AI-native or make universal claims about speed, cost, or productivity without an agreed baseline and evidence.

## Validation

The message will be tested in ten qualified buyer conversations. Buyer language and offer resonance will guide revisions. The existing ICP is sufficient for those tests; creating another ICP document would not produce market evidence.

## Next Action

Apply the approved message hierarchy to brand identity and minimum website copy while beginning buyer conversations.
