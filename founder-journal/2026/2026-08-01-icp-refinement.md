# ICP Refinement: 1 August 2026

## Context

The proposed customer profile covered startups through large enterprises, 20–500 engineers, many industries, and several regions. That range described the eventual market but did not focus first-client outreach.

## Decision

The initial beachhead hypothesis is software-intensive B2B product companies with roughly 20–150 engineers, an accountable CTO or engineering leader, and an urgent architecture, modernisation, delivery-system, or responsible-AI problem suited to a paid assessment or focused sprint.

Large enterprises and regulated sectors are not excluded permanently, but they are not default first-client targets because procurement, proof, and stakeholder requirements can extend the sales cycle.

Opportunities must pass six qualification gates. Accounts scoring at least 11 of 14 with no disqualifier receive priority.

## Evidence Plan

Review the hypothesis after 10 qualified buyer conversations and approve or revise it after 20. Geography, price, and offer emphasis will be selected from recorded buyer evidence rather than founder preference.

## Next Action

Build the first qualified-account list and test the ICP and UVP in live conversations. The existing service catalogue is sufficient to start; packaging the three launch offers into buyer-facing one-pagers is the next commercial content task.
