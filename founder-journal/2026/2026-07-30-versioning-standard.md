# Founder Journal — Engineering Versioning Standard

**Date:** 30 July 2026  
**Stage:** Engineering Governance

## Topics Discussed

- Semantic versioning for engineering artefacts.
- Separating lifecycle status from document version.
- Revision history, compatibility, supersession, governance, and AI assistance.
- Transitioning from standards to authoritative references.

## Founder Input

The founder selected `STD-VERSIONING` as the third governance pillar after traceability and metadata.

## Key Insights

- Version communicates change significance; status communicates maturity and authority.
- The impact on users determines the increment, not the size of the edit.
- Approval does not require a version increment unless content changes.
- Replacements and new versions must preserve distinct identity and history.

## Decisions

- Published `STD-VERSIONING` v1.0.0 as Draft.
- Removed the generic versioning placeholder to avoid duplicate sources of truth.
- Prioritised `REF-IDENTIFIERS`, `REF-TERMINOLOGY`, and `REF-ACRONYMS` as the next work.
