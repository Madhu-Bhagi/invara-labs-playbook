# Data Engineering Playbook — 30 July 2026

## Decision

Create `PB-DATA-ENGINEERING` as the third Enterprise Engineering Capability and govern data as an engineering product.

## Outcome

- Defined Data Engineering beyond ETL, databases, and tool-specific guidance.
- Created a nine-phase workflow from consumer need and source discovery through operation, evolution, and retirement.
- Established data contracts, models, quality, metadata, lineage, governance, security, privacy, and platform operations.
- Linked Data Engineering to Platform Engineering, Security Engineering, AI Engineering, and the core lifecycle.
- Deferred API Management and Developer Experience until separately authored.

## Status

🟡 In Progress — the playbook is authored as Draft v1.0.0 and requires formal review and approval.
