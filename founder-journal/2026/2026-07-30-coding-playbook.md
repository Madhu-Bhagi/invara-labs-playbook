- Added twelve enterprise appendices for execution governance and review readiness.
- Expanded v1.1.0 to 34 sections with first-class coding governance, risk management, secure coding, human accountability, and Standards integration.
- Made documentation an explicit implementation phase and aligned the appendices with production-readiness handoff.
