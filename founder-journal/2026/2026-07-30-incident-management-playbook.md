# Incident Management Playbook — 30 July 2026

## Context

The Operations Engineering layer needed a coordinated response process for material production impact.

## Decision

Create `PB-INCIDENT-MANAGEMENT` as a modern engineering incident-response SOP rather than a generic IT service-management process.

The playbook:

- Consumes detection and evidence from `PB-OBSERVABILITY`.
- Establishes early declaration, severity, incident command, roles, communication, and escalation.
- Prioritises protection, containment, restoration, and stability before causal analysis.
- Separates mitigation, recovery, resolution, review, and closure.
- Governs systemic RCA, corrective and preventive action, and knowledge capture.
- Preserves human incident authority when AI assists.

Specialised incident, alerting, communication, severity, RCA, postmortem, and security authorities remain planned and non-authoritative until separately approved.

## Outcome

`PB-INCIDENT-MANAGEMENT` v1.0.0 is a Draft with 38 sections, ten workflow phases, five quality gates, and twelve enterprise appendices.

Review and approve `PB-INCIDENT-MANAGEMENT`, then author `PB-CHANGE-MANAGEMENT` against its improvement handoff.
