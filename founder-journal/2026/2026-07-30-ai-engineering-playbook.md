# Founder Journal — AI Engineering Playbook

**Date:** 30 July 2026  
**Stage:** Playbook Authoring

## Context

AI already assists architecture and technical design, but phase-specific guidance does not provide an organisation-wide operating procedure for tool approval, context handling, verification, security, review, and contribution traceability.

## Decisions

- Replaced the AI-Assisted Development placeholder and adopted `PB-AI-ENGINEERING` v1.1.0 as the canonical Draft identity.
- Positioned it as a foundational, cross-lifecycle playbook before Coding.
- Expanded it to 33 sections with dedicated AI governance, security and privacy, approved-tool, prompt-engineering, and human-verification guidance.
- Refined v1.2.0 to a 32-section SOP focused on process and governance rather than tool or prompt instruction.
- Separated changeable tool approval into planned `REF-AI-TOOLS`.
- Separated reusable prompting practices into planned `REF-PROMPT-PATTERNS`.
- Elevated v1.3.0 into the enterprise Engineering AI Operating Procedure.
- Added SDLC-wide AI assistance, explicit AI risk management, intellectual-property controls, human accountability, approved capability classes, and twelve flagship appendices.
- Operationalised all six approved AI Engineering Principles.
- Required safe and minimal context, task-specific tool approval, independent verification, security review, human disposition, and proportionate traceability.
- Kept the actual tool allowlist in an authoritative operational register rather than embedding volatile vendor choices in the playbook.
- Prohibited AI from approving output, accepting risk, bypassing delivery controls, or independently authorising production action.
- Added enterprise risk, tool-approval, code-review, testing, incident-response, metrics, and maturity controls.
