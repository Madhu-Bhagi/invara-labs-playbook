# Founder Journal — Engineering Traceability Standard

**Date:** 30 July 2026  
**Stage:** Engineering Governance

## Topics Discussed

- End-to-end traceability across engineering artefacts.
- Mandatory identifiers, ownership, relationships, and link maintenance.
- Traceability governance and AI-assisted analysis.
- Metadata as the next automation dependency.

## Founder Input

The founder selected `STD-TRACEABILITY` as a cornerstone Engineering Operating System standard.

## Key Insights

- Traceability turns isolated documents into a connected engineering knowledge system.
- Traceability depth must remain proportionate to business value, risk, lifetime, and regulatory needs.
- A traceability matrix must not become a second source of truth.
- AI may suggest relationships, but engineers remain accountable for acceptance.

## Decisions

- Published `STD-TRACEABILITY` v1.0.0 as Draft.
- Added `STD-METADATA` to the planned backlog rather than creating it without a reviewed specification.
