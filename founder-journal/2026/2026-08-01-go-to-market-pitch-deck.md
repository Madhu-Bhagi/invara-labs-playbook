# Go-to-Market Pitch Deck — 1 August 2026

## Decision

Move from internal company-strategy authoring to a client-ready Go-to-Market sprint. The first external asset is the Invara Labs Company Pitch Deck; the Capability Statement follows.

## Rationale

The approved Vision, Strategy Pack, ICP, Launch Service Catalogue, and Website content already provide sufficient source material. A reusable presentation creates immediate value in buyer, partner, and investor conversations without inventing proof or duplicating strategy.

## Delivered

- A 12-slide presentation covering the buyer problem, beachhead customer, positioning, three launch offers, engagement method, Engineering Operating System, trust model, and call to action.
- Source citations in speaker notes for factual and positioning claims.
- Visual and automated overflow validation across all slides.

## Boundaries

- No invented customers, testimonials, outcomes, awards, or market-leadership claims.
- No AI-first or AI-native positioning.
- Exact pricing remains evidence-led and is not asserted in the deck.

## Next Action

Create a concise Capability Statement derived from the same approved sources, then test both assets in qualified buyer conversations.
