# Website V1 Content: 1 August 2026

## Context

The company has enough internal strategy to present a credible public offer. The proposed website included nine routes, broad technology and industry claims, AI-native language, a free strategy session, and services not yet validated.

## Decision

Website V1 will contain five routes: Home, Services, Framework, About, and Contact. It will market the three approved launch offers and use the approved engineering-first, AI-enabled position.

Solutions, Industries, Accelerators, Insights, and Case Studies will be added only when real content or evidence exists. The site will not publish unverified technology breadth, customer proof, certifications, testimonials, or performance claims.

The primary call to action is “Discuss an engineering challenge.” Qualification is free; the assessment itself is paid.

## Next Action

Build the five-route website, connect a secure contact destination, verify accessibility and metadata, and keep the public repository link hidden until release review is complete.
