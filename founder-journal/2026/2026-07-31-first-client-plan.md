# First-Client Execution Decision — 31 July 2026

## Context

After completing broad Draft coverage of the Engineering Operating System, the discussion shifted from producing documentation to building a sustainable company.

## Decision

The next company milestone is a signed, paid client engagement. A 90-day execution plan will direct work toward sellable consulting packages, a credible website, public proof, targeted outreach, and one reusable accelerator.

The existing **engineering-first, AI-enabled, business-focused** positioning remains authoritative. The proposed “AI-first” identity was not adopted because it conflicts with the approved foundation and could make a durable engineering company depend on a changing technology label.

Consulting is the immediate revenue engine. Products and education support delivery leverage and demand generation. Proprietary platform investment waits for repeated customer evidence.

## Consequences

- No additional playbooks or governance frameworks during the 90-day plan unless required to remove a commercial blocker.
- Outreach begins before the website, repository, and accelerator are perfect.
- Weekly priorities are tested against client acquisition, reputation, or reusable commercial value.
- The founder retains decision authority; AI acts as a strategic and technical adviser.
