## Context

The repository has moved from structure design to substantive authoring. `PB-ARCH` establishes the approved system direction; the next lifecycle step must translate that direction into a design engineers can implement.

- Replaced the Technical Design placeholder with `PB-TECH-DESIGN` v1.0.0 as Draft.

- Established it as the second flagship ILOS playbook and reference pattern for execution-ready delivery guidance.
- Defined nine phases from understanding approved architecture through coding-ready handover.
- Required component, contract, data, security, failure, observability, configuration, deployment, and implementation-plan coverage.
- Prevented technical design from silently changing approved architecture; material deviations return to `PB-ARCH`.
- Kept AI in an assistive role while preserving human responsibility for correctness, risk, review, and approval.
- Added reusable templates, quality gates, enterprise checklists, traceability, a maturity model, and a coding-readiness gate.
