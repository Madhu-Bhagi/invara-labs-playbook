# Platform Engineering Playbook — 30 July 2026

## Decision

Begin the Enterprise Engineering Capabilities layer with `PB-PLATFORM-ENGINEERING`.

## Outcome

- Defined Platform Engineering as an internal-product discipline rather than a technology guide.
- Created a ten-phase workflow from user discovery through operation, measurement, evolution, and retirement.
- Established golden paths, self-service, platform products, developer experience, security, governance, adoption, and lifecycle controls.
- Linked Platform Engineering to the existing engineering and operations lifecycle.
- Deferred Security Engineering, Data Engineering, API Management, and Developer Experience playbooks until separately authored.

## Status

🟡 In Progress — the playbook is authored as Draft v1.0.0 and requires formal review and approval.
