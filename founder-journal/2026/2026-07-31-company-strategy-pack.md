# Company Strategy Pack Decision: 31 July 2026

## Context

The first-client plan established a revenue milestone but did not yet answer why a buyer should choose Invara Labs, which buyers to pursue, or what they could purchase.

## Decision

Invara Labs will compete as an engineering excellence company. The approved Company Strategy Pack defines five service capabilities and limits the launch focus to three offers: Engineering Excellence Assessment, Modernisation Accelerator, and AI Engineering Adoption Sprint.

The assessment is the primary paid entry offer. Platform Engineering and EEOS Implementation remain expansion services until buyer evidence supports standalone packaging.

Outreach begins immediately. The website, public EEOS material, and Enterprise Reference Implementation #1: Angular support credibility and conversion but are not prerequisites for buyer conversations.

## Open Evidence

The founder must validate the primary geography, company-size band, buyer segment, price floor, and strongest pain trigger through market conversations. The strategy deliberately does not invent these answers.
