# Change Management Playbook — 30 July 2026

## Context

The Operations Engineering layer needed risk-proportionate governance between engineering intent, production deployment, and operational learning.

## Decision

Create `PB-CHANGE-MANAGEMENT` as an Engineering Change Governance SOP rather than a paperwork-heavy ITIL process.

The playbook:

- Classifies Standard, Normal, and Emergency changes.
- Matches evidence, review, and approval to impact and uncertainty.
- Separates Change approval from Deployment execution and Incident response.
- Governs scheduling, conflicts, communication, validation, rollback, recovery, and closure.
- Preserves human approval and risk authority when AI assists.
- Uses outcome learning to improve automation and Standard Change models.

Specialised change, risk, security, communication, approval, and rollback authorities remain planned and non-authoritative until separately approved.

## Outcome

`PB-CHANGE-MANAGEMENT` v1.0.0 is a Draft with 38 sections, nine workflow phases, five quality gates, and twelve enterprise appendices.

`PB-MAINTENANCE` is now registered as the final planned core lifecycle playbook.
