# Security Engineering Playbook — 30 July 2026

## Decision

Create `PB-SECURITY-ENGINEERING` as the second Enterprise Engineering Capability and replace the overlapping planned generic Security Playbook.

## Outcome

- Defined Security Engineering as a cross-lifecycle engineering discipline rather than an InfoSec policy or control catalogue.
- Created a nine-phase workflow from asset context and threat modelling through secure operation, vulnerability treatment, and improvement.
- Integrated secure design, development, verification, supply chain, identity, secrets, monitoring, incidents, and risk governance.
- Linked Security Engineering to Platform Engineering and the full engineering lifecycle.
- Deferred Data Engineering, API Management, and Developer Experience until separately authored.

## Status

🟡 In Progress — the playbook is authored as Draft v1.0.0 and requires formal review and approval.
