# Engineering Governance Framework — 31 July 2026

## Decision

Pause playbook expansion and create `EGF-ENGINEERING-GOVERNANCE` as the governance layer above engineering Principles, Playbooks, Standards, References, Templates, and Examples.

## Outcome

- Defined decision rights, governance levels, roles, optional councils, domain governance, exceptions, risk, assurance, metrics, and review cadence.
- Kept engineering governance separate from ILOS documentation governance.
- Placed the framework at the Engineering domain root instead of restructuring `docs/`.
- Recorded ADR-009 and registered the `EGF` identifier family.
- Deferred the Maturity Model, Metrics Framework, Technology Radar, and Cross-reference Matrix as separate governed artefacts.

## Status

🟡 In Progress — Draft v1.0.0 requires formal review, approval, and named delegated authorities.
