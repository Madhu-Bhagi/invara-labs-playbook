# Launch Service Catalogue: 1 August 2026

## Context

The internal Strategy Pack named five service capabilities, but the first-client motion needed a buyer-facing explanation of what can be purchased now.

## Decision

The launch catalogue markets three offers only: Engineering Excellence Assessment, Modernisation Accelerator, and AI Engineering Adoption Sprint. Each has a bounded entry point, intended outcome, delivery path, deliverables, client responsibilities, exclusions, and commercial model.

Platform Engineering and EEOS Implementation remain expansion services. A broader value ladder containing low-cost products, courses, fractional CTO services, platform ownership, or large transformations is not a committed roadmap until repeated demand and delivery evidence justify it.

## Commercial Principle

Invara Labs sells outcomes and accountable engineering judgement rather than technologies or developer headcount. Outcome claims require engagement-specific baselines and evidence.

## Next Action

Use the catalogue in buyer conversations and translate the approved UVP, ICP, and offers into minimum website content.
